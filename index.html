Format

  • Every team plays against every other team twice — Home & Away.
  • Games last 50 minutes — 25 minutes per half.
  • Win = 3 pts  ·  Draw = 1 pt each  ·  Loss = 0 pts.
  • Top team after the 2nd leg is declared Champions .
  • Winners receive a trophy ; all players are awarded medals .
  • League facilitation fee: USD 200 — paid once.
  • Game fee: USD 35 per team, per match.
