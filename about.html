About Us

LiveZone Football is a 6-a-side football league that integrates participating teams in an organised, competitive setting based in Juba, South Sudan. Interested teams are registered and a full season schedule of fixtures with their respective dates is drafted before play begins.

Organised Fixtures

Every team plays against all other teams twice — Home and Away. Games are officiated by a referee provided by the league, ensuring fair play throughout the season.

Live Statistics

Stats are recorded during every game and posted on the league website and social media platforms — standings, results, fixtures, and top scorers, all up to date.

Competitive Spirit

Games are fun but fiercely competitive. Champions are crowned at the end of the season — the top team lifts the trophy and every player is awarded a medal.

Community First

At LiveZone Football we embrace the cohesive nature of the global game. Football brings people together — and that is exactly what we are here to do in Juba.
